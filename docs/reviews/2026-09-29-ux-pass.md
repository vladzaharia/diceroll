# Diceroll UX pass: findings and fixes

Reviewer: UX/UI designer pass. Date: 2026-09-29. Build: `main` at 1308a07.

## Method

- **Screens:** 105 UI scenarios, listed from `tools/scenarios.gd` and every provider it registers. These are the title, class select, run setup, board, combat and every modal, the dev menu, results, the Camp and its five stations, all 11 minigames, and the missing-assets boot screen. Galleries, icons and actor-only scenarios were left out.
- **Devices:** each scenario was shot on seven devices:
  - desktop 1920x1080 and 1366x768;
  - iPhone 17 portrait (402x874, `--safe` notch);
  - iPhone Duo outer (466x678) and inner (626x890);
  - iPad mini portrait;
  - iPad Pro 13 landscape.

  A zoom and DPI set covered six key screens: 1080p at 1.5x, 1366 at 1.25x, iPhone at 1.25x and iPhone at @2x.
- **Numbers audit:** `tools/shot.gd` gained an `--audit` flag. It lists visible text under 16 canvas px and buttons under 80 canvas px (44 pt on a 402-pt phone). It ran on every scenario on the iPhone, before and after the fixes.
- **Where the files are:** screenshots are in `build/ux-pass/{before,after}/<scenario>__<device>.png` (gitignored, local to the main checkout). Contact sheets are in `build/ux-pass/sheets/`.

Severity scale:
- **blocker:** breaks a flow or hides essential information.
- **major:** a player will notice it and be confused or annoyed.
- **minor:** a visible flaw with a workaround.
- **polish:** a nice-to-have.

## Rule text (finding 3)

`ItemDefs.rule_text` now does the whole job; the picker shows its output as-is (`ArmoryModal.rule_text` and `_fill` are gone).

- **Tier clauses.** A "tier II:" or "tier III:" clause reads with that tier's own numbers. For example: Coin Purse "tier III: cash-outs +25%", Healer's Flask "tier III: potions heal +3%".
- **New template placeholders:**
  - `{key|times}` gives once / twice / N times;
  - `{key|turns1}` gives turn 1 / turns 1-N;
  - `{key|die/dice}` gives 1 die / N dice;
  - `{key+%}` shows a factor's bonus as +N%. Spear: "deals +2% damage (+5% against a final boss)". Warhammer: "gets +15% pips".
- **Oath Shield** reads "Block equal to 10% of the set's value".
- **Numbers.** No rule numbers or balance values changed; only `desc` strings and the formatter.
- **Test.** `tests/test_armory_ui.gd::test_picker_rule_text` now checks `ItemDefs.rule_text` for `{`, `+0%`, `(s)`, `turns 1-1`, `1 dice`, `1 times` and `x<digit>`, across every item and tier.
